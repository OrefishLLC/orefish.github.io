Our Games

One More Push

An absurd little climb

Push a boulder up an endless mountain. A calm, funny incremental game about the one task that never quite ends: climb toward the summit, hold your balance at the top, and start over. Between runs, stack a cairn or skip a stone, decorate your boulder and your collection of stones, and watch the day roll into night across a handful of worlds.

  • Standard and Endless climbs, with tap, rhythm, or timing controls
  • A balance challenge waiting at every summit
  • Side games between climbs — stack cairns, skip stones, keep a trail of monuments
  • Decorate your boulder and stone collection with skins, stickers & bands
  • Earn achievements that unlock new cosmetics
  • Living day/night skies, weather, and auroras across multiple worlds
  • No ads, no tracking, plays offline

About Orefish LLC

Founded in 2020, Orefish, LLC is a Florida limited liability company that builds small, polished games and apps for iOS. We work on a few titles at a time and focus on getting each one right.

How We Build

Every Orefish title aims to be:

  • P
    Privacy-First: No ads and no third-party ad tracking. One More Push and our small games collect no personal data.
  • O
    Plays Offline: Full gameplay without an internet connection
  • C
    Crafted: Hand-built and properly finished, not rushed out
  • R
    Reliable: Thoroughly tested and maintained
