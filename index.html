Our Games

One More Push

An absurd little climb

Push a boulder up an endless mountain. A calm, funny incremental game about the one task that never quite ends — climb toward the summit, balance at the top, and start again. Decorate your boulder, watch the day turn to night, and keep pushing.

  • Endless and Standard climbs
  • Tap, rhythm, and timing controls
  • Decorate your boulder with skins & stickers
  • Living day/night skies and auroras
  • No ads, no tracking, plays offline

About Orefish LLC

Founded in 2020, Orefish, LLC is a Florida limited liability company that builds small, polished applications and games for iOS. We focus on a handful of titles made with care, rather than many made quickly.

How We Build

Every Orefish title aims to be:

  • P
    Privacy-First: No ads and no third-party ad tracking. One More Push and our small games collect no data at all.
  • O
    Plays Offline: Full gameplay without an internet connection
  • C
    Crafted: Small games made deliberately and finished properly
  • R
    Reliable: Thoroughly tested and maintained
