Our Games

One More Push

An absurd little climb

Push a boulder up an endless mountain. A calm, funny incremental game about the one task that never quite ends: climb toward the summit, hold your balance at the top, and start over. Between runs, stack a cairn or skip a stone, decorate your boulder and your collection of stones, and watch the day roll into night across a handful of worlds.

  • Standard and Endless climbs, with tap, rhythm, or timing controls
  • A balance challenge waiting at every summit
  • Mini games such as stone skipping, stacking, and boulder balancing
  • Decorate your boulder and stone collection with skins, stickers & bands
  • Earn achievements that unlock new cosmetics
  • Living day/night skies, weather, and auroras across multiple worlds
  • No ads, no tracking, and offline play

About Orefish

Orefish is a small iOS studio that, these days, is really just one person. It didn't start that way. In 2020 it was a team with a big idea: a card game you could actually play with other people on your phone. Over the next few years that team grew to eight and, over time, drifted back down. By 2025 it was me on my own, with nothing shipped yet.

So I kept building. Delve Divers is that original idea, still going after more than two years of solo work: a deckbuilder in the Slay the Spire vein, reaching toward something more multiplayer and alive. One More Push came later and much smaller, a calm game I could finish and put in front of people while Delve Divers comes together. You can see all of One More Push here; Delve Divers has its own site.

I build these around a full-time career in compliance, mostly on nights and weekends. No publisher, no investors, no ads, and no tracking. Just small games that get finished and shipped when they're ready.
