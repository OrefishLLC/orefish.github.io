One More Push: Boulder Climb

The impossible summit awaits

As Sisyphus did, you push a boulder up a mountain toward an impossible goal. This time, the journey is yours. Travel across seven lands of fantasy and magic, from green foothills to frozen peaks, burning calderas, and starlit voids. Earn Pebbles with every push and spend them on treasures for your only companion in this world: your Boulder.

  • Standard and Endless climbs, with tap or rhythm controls
  • A balance challenge at every summit: tilt your phone to steer, or swap to touch any time
  • Seven living worlds: begin on Green Mountain, then unlock six realms, each with its own weather, music, and life. Auroras over the frozen peaks, embers, marsh wisps, sea spray, rising light, and falling star dust
  • More than 500 treasures to earn: stickers, bands, and stones, from a humble olive sprig to the Realm Relics
  • Camp mini games: stone skipping, stone stacking, and boulder balancing
  • Achievements that unlock cosmetics of their own
  • An original synthesized soundtrack and living ambience: crickets, distant thunder, wind that actually moves
  • Full offline play with no account required; every purchase is cosmetic and plainly priced
