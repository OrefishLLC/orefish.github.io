Frequently Asked Questions

1

Does One More Push collect my data?

No. Our games collect no personal data and have no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. Everything you do stays on your device and your own iCloud.

2

How is my progress saved and synced?

Your progress is saved on your device and synced through your personal iCloud, so it appears automatically on your other devices signed in to the same Apple ID. Nothing is ever sent to us.

3

How do in-app purchases work?

Some games offer optional one-time purchases of cosmetic items, such as boulder skins and stickers. Purchases are processed by Apple, tied to your Apple ID, and are permanent once bought. There are no randomized or "loot box" items — you always know exactly what you're getting.

4

I reinstalled the app or got a new device. How do I get my purchases back?

Open the game's Settings and tap "Restore Purchases" while signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy them. Your cosmetic items will be restored at no additional cost.

5

Can I get a refund?

Refunds for in-app purchases are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

6

I found a bug or have a suggestion.

We'd love to hear it. Email support@orefish.com with as much detail as you can, and we'll usually reply within 48 hours.

7

Where can I read the privacy policy and terms?

They're linked inside the app and here on our site: see our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

8

I'm looking for help with Delve Divers.

Delve Divers is a separate product with its own support, accounts, and policies. Please visit delvedivers.com for Delve Divers support — this page and these policies don't cover it.
