Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the games published by Orefish LLC ("we," "our," or "us"), including One More Push. The short version: we do not collect your personal data, and we have no accounts, no ads, and no analytics.

1. Information We Don't Collect

Our games do not collect, store, transmit, or have access to your personal information, identifiers, contacts, location, or usage analytics. We do not maintain servers that receive data from you, and we cannot see how you play.

2. Where Your Progress Is Stored

Your in-game progress — settings, in-game currency, cosmetic items, statistics, and streaks — is saved locally on your device. To keep your progress consistent across your own Apple devices, it is also synced through your personal iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key-value storage. This data stays within your Apple account; it is never sent to us and we cannot access it. You can remove it at any time from within the game or through your device and iCloud settings.

3. In-App Purchases

Some games offer optional in-app purchases (for example, cosmetic items). All purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive or have access to your payment information. We receive only the anonymous confirmation needed to unlock the item you bought. Apple maintains the record of your purchases on your Apple ID, which is what allows you to restore them on your devices. Apple's handling of purchase data is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

4. No Analytics, Advertising, or Tracking

We do not use analytics services, advertising networks, tracking pixels, software development kits that collect data, or any form of user monitoring. We do not track your activity across apps or websites, and we do not request the App Tracking Transparency permission because we have nothing to track.

5. No Accounts

Our games do not require or offer account creation, login, registration, or any form of personal identification.

6. Network Use

Our games are designed to be played offline. The only network activity involves Apple's own services — syncing your progress through your iCloud and processing any in-app purchases through the App Store — and opening external links that you choose to tap, such as this policy, our support page, or the App Store.

7. Children's Privacy

Our games are suitable for general audiences and collect no data from anyone, including children under 13. Because we collect no personal information, we comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws by design.

8. Third-Party Links

Our games and this website may contain links to external websites, such as the App Store or our own pages. Those destinations have their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

9. Data Security

Because we collect no data, there is nothing for us to secure on any server. Data stored on your device is protected by your device's built-in security, and data synced through iCloud is protected by your Apple account.

10. California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

We do not collect, sell, or share personal information, and therefore have no personal information to disclose, delete, or correct in response to a request under California law.

11. European & UK Privacy Rights (GDPR/UK GDPR)

We do not collect or process personal data, so the GDPR and UK GDPR impose no processing obligations on us. Any data handled by Apple in connection with iCloud sync or App Store purchases is processed by Apple as a separate controller under Apple's policies.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version is always indicated by the "Last Updated" date above.
